04 / Research direction

When a detector's world shifts — is it nature, or an adversary?

Deployed models drift. Some drift is natural — new customers, new seasons. Some is deliberate — attackers probing the edges. Having built fraud detectors in production, I want to study how to tell the two apart, and how vision and security systems can adapt without being steered.

THREAD 01

Adversarial vs. natural drift

Signals that separate deliberate manipulation from organic distribution change.
THREAD 02

Robust computer vision

Face and document models that stay reliable under spoofing and real-world noise.
THREAD 03

AI security in practice

Secure, privacy-respecting deployment of ML and LLM tools in small organizations.
